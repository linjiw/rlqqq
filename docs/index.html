Mechanism and limits

How the policy decides

A volatility budget sets risk first. The learned ensemble makes a bounded residual adjustment rather than choosing an unconstrained trade.

  1. 01

    Size the risk budget

    Trailing 21-day volatility maps to a 20% target, capped at 1.5x exposure.

    VT20 = min(1.5, 20% / realized vol)
  2. 02

    Read the learned tilt

    Ten v4 PPO seeds are averaged. Their exposure relative to the 10% target becomes a bounded multiplier.

    tilt = clip(agent / VT10, 0.5, 1.5)
  3. 03

    Apply and account

    The tilt modifies VT20; one-way trading costs and financing above 1.0x are deducted daily.

    target = min(1.5, tilt x VT20)

Evaluation design

  • Eight anchored test folds spanning 2010-2025.
  • Twenty-one trading-day embargoes between validation and test.
  • Ten-seed mean-exposure ensemble; no best-seed selection.
  • QQQ and SPY evaluated on the same 3,838 sampled dates.
  • Two-basis-point one-way cost and T-bill + 50 bp financing.

Known limitations

  • The headline composite was constructed post-hoc from test-fold series.
  • Its Sharpe and CAGR confidence intervals both cross zero.
  • There are 162 omitted embargo days; this is a stitched OOS replay.
  • The deployable bundle contains actor weights, not resumable PPO checkpoints.
  • The repository bootstrap helper mixes raw and excess Sharpe conventions.
  • DCA is excluded because its current accounting is not true periodic contribution accounting.
