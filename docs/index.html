RLQQQ strategy design

The model does not select stocks or predict a price. It adjusts how much QQQ the portfolio holds for the next session.

A volatility anchor sets the base

The base position targets 20% annualized volatility: when markets are calm it holds more QQQ (up to 1.5× with financed leverage), and when volatility spikes it automatically de-risks. This rule alone is a strong benchmark.

Ten RL agents adjust the risk

Ten independently trained PPO agents read 28 market features (momentum, volatility, trend, volume, VIX, yield curve, bonds, gold) and each votes a multiplier from 0.5× to 1.5× on the anchor. The deployed decision is the average of the ten.

One target becomes the portfolio

The ten targets are averaged. Up to 1.0×, the balance earns the T-bill rate in cash; above 1.0×, extra QQQ exposure is financed at T-bill plus 50 bp. Trading costs are included.

The honest fine print

Research and paper-trading signal only — not investment advice, not an execution order, and not qualified for capital deployment: under a conservative one-day execution lag, a simple volatility rule matches the model. Backtests assume same-close fills.

The 2010–2025 window was used during research; the 2000–2009 stress test and the live 2026 record are the out-of-development evidence. Sharpe differences vs buy & hold are statistically significant only in the stress decade.

Model weights are frozen (SHA-256 pinned); the browser recomputes every decision from published raw inputs and shows it only when it matches an independent Python reference bit-for-bit. Full audit trail on the research console; code and 2,000+ logged experiments on GitHub.
